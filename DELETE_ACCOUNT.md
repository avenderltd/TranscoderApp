# Account & Data Deletion Request — Pro Media Studio (Avender Ltd)

Users of **Pro Media Studio / AI Video Dubber** by **Avender Ltd** can request the complete deletion of their Studio Cloud account and associated data at any time:

### How to Request Account Deletion
1. **In-App:** Open the app, tap **Cloud Account, Plans & AdMob**, sign in, and tap **Delete Account**, OR
2. **By Email:** Send an email to **info@avenderltd.com** with the subject line **"Delete My Studio Cloud Account"** from the email address registered to your account.

### Data Deleted & Retention Period
* **Deleted Immediately (within 48 hours of request):** Your registered email address, Firebase Authentication user ID (`uid`), and Cloud Quota balance record.
* **Local Media Files:** All videos, audio tracks, and subtitles are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to Avender Ltd servers.
